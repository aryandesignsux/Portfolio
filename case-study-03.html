Case study 03 · Lending

Improving the Onboarding Funnel at Olyv.

My role
Research, Design, Dev Handoff
Team
1 PM, 1 Researcher, 1 Designer
Timeline
2 weeks
Overview

At Olyv, we cater to 3 Lakh+ Daily Active Users on the application. For a fintech application, onboarding is a necessity.

Our App onboarding Flow went through multiple changes over the years (due to compliance, tech limitations, business metrics, data privacy, etc) making it very cognitive load heavy for the user.

Problem

Reduce Drop-offs in the Onboarding Flow.

A user onboarding on the app was expected to complete 9 steps before seeing 1st sign of Personal Loan. This had to be corrected.

Research

Onboarding Flow Competitor Benchmarking.

First step in research was to understand how other lending & investment apps were onboarding their users.

We identified pointers that we believed could be implemented in our onboarding flow too. The shortlisted ones were:

01Carousal informed users about primary USPs of the application.
02OTP got auto-filled & verified automatically for a few apps.
03Email had a lot more options than just traditional input field.
04PAN was asked in later steps of the journey.
05PIN setup was skipped by using mobile phone's facial recognition or fingerprint verification.
Research

Talking to Real Users to know how they onboarded.

We went to local vendors in Kadubeesanahalli & Panathur who resembled our user base so we can see them onboarding on the app firsthand. We found a mix of users who were had prior experience of taking loans from Quick Loan Apps, & ones who were unfamiliar of such apps.

We understood mental models of both such users and found out common insights. They were:

01They were used to Phone Number & its OTP Verification.
02People who'd used similar apps prior, knew to keep physical PAN Card handy.
03Almost 60% of them got confused on Referral Page.
04They identified PAN Details page via PAN illustration on top.
05A few users had a hard time differentiating b/w Setting a PIN & Confirming the same PIN.
Research

Identifying Problems in the Existing Flow.

We analysed points that could be improved screen by screen to reduce cognitive load on the user.

Define

What we decided to work upon

It was important for us to release this fast as this was causing a big drop in the onboarding to unlock funnel. 26 thousand daily new users were impacted.

Design

Redesigning the flow from scratch

We scrapped the existing flow entirely and rebuilt from first principles. The new flow introduces a soft eligibility check upfront, a document checklist before data entry begins, and a persistent progress indicator throughout.

Login PIN Setup

This is to prevent misuse by people other than the user by accessing the application.

Step 1: Carousal

Three slides covering the app's primary offerings: Loans, Credit Health and Digital Gold Savings

We didn't change the carousal. Only the consent was moved from this page to later pages.

Step 2: Phone Number & OTP

Phone number entry, OTP entry, and the fallback state when the OTP does not arrive.

Improvements
01

Progress bar

Current stage is visible at every step of the flow.

02

Help icon on every page

With FAQs that change based on the screen the user is on.

03

OTP sent reconfirmation

A snackbar confirms the OTP went out, and by which channel.

04

Edit phone number

Correcting a mistyped number no longer means restarting the flow.

Step 3: PAN Details Verification

PAN number, name and date of birth, followed by verification and its success state.

Improvements
01

Pre-filled PAN details

Fetched via the DigiTap API instead of typed out by the user.

02

Automatic progression

The user moves ahead on their own once NSDL verification succeeds.

03

Age shown below DOB

A readback of the entered date, to cut down DOB mismatch errors.

Futurescope

What we need to change further

There are still a few things I want to improve going forward:

01

PIN Page Revamp

Currently, the dependency on PIN page from security standpoint delayed its change. However, we were able to implement Phone Biometric Activation at that step.

02

PAN Details being pre-filled only after NSDL verification

Currently, digitap API fetches user's PAN details, they get pre-filled & user edits them once they see an error message. PAN should only be pre-filled for users whose details have been verified by NSDL.

03

Phone No. OTP autofetch

Phone Number OTP can be auto-fetched & verified so we can reduce 1 step for the user.
